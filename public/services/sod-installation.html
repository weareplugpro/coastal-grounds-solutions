01 / 03

Pulling the old turf

Step one is stripping the dead or patchy turf, plus the thatch and debris beneath it. Then low spots are filled and high spots shaved, so rain runs toward the street rather than pooling in the middle of the lawn, and the new sod sits flush with your walks and driveway.

Rows go down tight with staggered seams, edges are cut around beds, walks and trees, and every piece is pressed firmly into the soil. That solid contact is what allows the new roots to grab hold.

02 / 03

Why bare patches happen

The usual culprits are harsh sun on thin grass, heavy shade, low ground that stays soggy, or a sprinkler that skips a corner. And because sand holds little water, a weak-rooted lawn shows stress quickly in the dry spring.

St. Augustine is the main lawn grass in Sarasota and Manatee County, though some yards have Bahia or zoysia instead. The right choice for your particular lot comes down to sun, shade and exposure to salt, and the free estimate works all of that out.

03 / 03

Caring for new sod early on

New roots need steady moisture while they knit into the soil, then less as they grow. Counties allow extra watering for freshly laid sod for a limited time, so ask your county’s water conservation office for the current days and hours.

Stay off it as much as possible, and skip mowing until the sod is rooted enough not to lift when tugged. You’ll get watering instructions on install day, and the crew sweeps up before leaving. After it roots, the lawn can move right onto a mowing schedule.

Can you replace only part of a lawn?

As long as the rest of the lawn is in good shape, yes. The crew removes the failed section, levels it and lays sod as close to your existing grass as possible. When most of the yard is thin or weedy, a full replacement usually looks better.

When can I walk on fresh sod?

Go easy for roughly two weeks while roots form, keeping kids and pets off when you can. Footsteps on fresh sod dent it and pull the seams apart. When a gentle tug won’t lift it, use the lawn as normal.

How much should I water new sod?

A lot at first, then less as the roots take over. Summer rain helps, yet sand dries quickly between storms, and a single sunny week can set new sod back. Follow the crew’s instructions and your county’s current watering rules.
