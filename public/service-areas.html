By town

Local notes for each town.

Each town’s neighborhoods, its yard troubles and how the crew deals with them.

Manatee County · about 23 min

Bradenton

Between the Manatee River and Palma Sola Bay, Bradenton has every kind of yard, and Coastal Grounds keeps houses and businesses there mowed and crisp.

 Manatee County · about 24 min

Palmetto

Marina communities, island lots and historic downtown streets make up Palmetto, across the Manatee River. Coastal Grounds mows, plants and tidies it all.

 Manatee County · about 26 min

Ellenton

Ellenton sits at the US 301 and I‑75 junction, so storefronts and river canals are close neighbors. Coastal Grounds serves residential and commercial properties across town.

 Manatee & Sarasota counties · about 24 min

Lakewood Ranch

Every Lakewood Ranch village runs its own HOA, and Coastal Grounds designs and maintains yards that satisfy those rules while still looking great from the street.

 Manatee County · about 39 min

Parrish

New neighborhoods keep rising on old farm and citrus land in fast-growing Parrish, and Coastal Grounds finishes the bare yards that builders leave behind.

 Sarasota County · home base

Sarasota

Coastal Grounds calls Sarasota home. Kevin Guerrero’s crew mows, plants and mulches from the bayfront all the way to the older bungalow streets.

 Sarasota County · about 19 min

Osprey

Little Sarasota Bay, the Intracoastal and Casey Key make Osprey mostly water and preserve, so Coastal Grounds plants yards that can take salt, wind and sand.

 Sarasota County · about 34 min

Venice

Planned in the 1920s around Italian-inspired buildings and wide avenues, Venice has yards Coastal Grounds tends from island cottages to Wellen Park lots.
