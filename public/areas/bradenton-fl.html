Bradenton // Manatee County

Riverfront to the bay.

South of downtown, the Village of the Arts has turned old bungalows into homes that double as studios and shops, thanks to live-work zoning. Off Manatee Avenue sits the historic Braden Castle Park district, and downtown the Riverwalk tracks the Manatee River. Tara offers golf-and-country-club living, while Palma Sola Harbour and Perico Bay Club face the water.

On a small bungalow lot, nothing hides, so beds need to be neat and edges sharp. Wind and salt air hit the bay-side streets, and a summer storm can float light mulch right out of a bed, which is why we put concrete curbing and weed barrier wherever water runs. In golf and retirement communities the association frequently handles part of the yard, and we take care of the homeowner’s share.

From downtown Sarasota it’s about 23 minutes north to Bradenton, and the crew works both the river side and the bay side.

How would you lay out a small bungalow lot near the Village of the Arts?

Think one curved bed edged in stone-look curbing, a handful of tropicals and a rock ring at the base of a palm. You mow less and enjoy more. The layout gets scaled to the lot, with paths wide enough to walk comfortably.

Can storm season wash out mulch beds close to the river?

Hard summer rain shifts light mulch, most of all on slopes and in low corners. The curbing holds the edge, weed barrier keeps soil from rising through the mulch, and rock in the wettest spots stays where it’s put, storm after storm.

In a retirement community? Can you handle the yard?

We handle mowing, trimming, mulch and cleanups. Look over your community’s rules first, since some associations maintain the common areas and approve plant changes. When you’re ready, call or text (239) 887-8922, and we’ll take care of your lot.
