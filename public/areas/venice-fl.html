Venice // Sarasota County

Island cottages to Wellen Park.

The original planned city, Venice Island, lines Venice Avenue with Mediterranean and Northern Italian buildings, has 1920s homes toward the Gulf, and includes historic districts such as Edgewood and Venezia Park. On the mainland are South Venice and Venice Gardens, and Humphris Park and the Venetian Waterway Park trail keep the Gulf and Intracoastal close. Wellen Park, with Downtown Wellen and Islandwalk, forms the town’s new master-planned edge.

Island and beach-side yards take salt air, so Gulf-facing properties do best with palms, rock beds and tough tropicals. Older lots often carry mature palms and hedges that need regular shaping. New Wellen Park homes arrive with builder sod and bare beds, usually under HOA and CDD rules. Some owners spend part of the year away, which makes seasonal cleanups important.

The farthest south of the crew’s eight towns, Venice is about 34 minutes from downtown Sarasota by way of US 41 or I‑75.

Which plants suit a Mediterranean-style home on Venice Island?

Stucco and stone pair well with palms, agave, bromeliads and rock beds. Keep the lines clean: symmetrical beds right at the entry, a stepping-stone path and a few focal palms, with enough open ground left to show off the columns and arches.

How many hedge trims does a summer take?

Growth peaks in the wet season, roughly June through September, and hedges turn shaggy quickly. An early trim plus one midway through holds the shape. Let us know how formal the hedge should be, and point out any palms whose fronds need cleaning up.

We’re away for part of each year. Can you keep up the yard?

Yes. Mowing, trimming and cleanups can follow your calendar. Share your departure and return dates, and ask about a spring or fall cleanup so the yard is ready when you get back.
