Completing what the builder skipped.

Around US 301, Rutland Road and Rye Road, newer communities include Aviary at Rutland Ranch, North River Ranch, Del Webb Sunchase and Prosperity Lakes. Aviary homesites typically measure about 50 or 60 feet across, and plenty back onto lakes or natural areas. Rye Preserve and the Florida Railroad Museum are nearby.

Expect thin builder sod, bare beds and zero shade on most of these lots. Young plants need steady water, and rock or mulch over weed barrier cuts weeding way down. When a yard backs up to a pond or preserve, plantings are kept low so the view stays open and the shoreline stays tidy.

By I‑75 and US 301, Parrish is about 39 minutes from downtown Sarasota and well within the regular service area.

New build, thin sod and empty beds. What do I do first?

Work from the front. Curbing defines the beds, weed barrier goes down, palms and shrubs go in, then the sod is patched or replaced. Handling beds and sod together avoids digging twice and keeps the yard from sitting half finished.

How can I add shade to a treeless lot?

For quick shade, plant palms and tall shrubs. Along the west side they cut afternoon heat on the house as they grow, and they fit the narrow side yards common on newer lots. Small palms from nursery pots go in at a size that’s easy to handle.
